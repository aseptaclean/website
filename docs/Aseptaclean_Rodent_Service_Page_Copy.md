## Different spaces need different cleanup plans.

Waste in an empty cabinet is one kind of job. Waste mixed into boxes, furniture, and stored items is another.

We look at both the space and the things in it.

### Kitchen cabinets, drawers, and pantries

You may find droppings on shelves, inside drawers, or near food and dishes.

We check the affected areas we can reach. We discuss what needs to come out so the space can be cleaned.

Your plan may include removing soiled shelf liners and materials, cleaning cabinet interiors, and disinfecting suitable hard surfaces.

We also discuss how to handle the items inside. Food, dishes, and packaging need different care.

### Garages and storage spaces

Waste may be along walls, beneath shelves, or mixed into stored belongings.

We discuss which items need to be moved to reach the waste. We also help you decide what can be cleaned and what needs to go.

A garage full of boxes may take more work than an empty garage of the same size. Your quote accounts for the space, the waste, and the items involved.

### Closets, furniture, and living areas

Rodent waste may affect floors, baseboards, furniture, clothing, and other belongings.

We check the material before choosing a cleaning method. A hard floor, fabric cushion, and paper item cannot all be treated the same way.

We explain what we can clean, what may need removal, and what needs special care.

### Several rooms or heavy rodent waste

When waste is spread across several areas, we make a plan for each space.

We discuss where to start, how to move waste out, and what steps will help protect nearby rooms and belongings.

Finding waste in one room does not mean every room needs the same work.

## What if rodents are still getting in?

Tell us when you call. Rodents can leave new waste after cleanup, so the timing matters.

A pest-control company can address the rodents and how they enter. Aseptaclean handles the cleanup.

Some pest-control companies also clean. Ask what their work includes so you know what has already been handled.

We do not:

- Trap rodents or treat pests.
- Seal holes where rodents get in.
- Replace insulation.
- Make structural repairs.
- Work on heating or air systems.

We remove soiled materials when they are part of your approved cleanup plan.

## Questions before you book.

### My pest-control company already cleaned. Do I still need you?

You may not.

Ask which areas they cleaned, what they removed, and what they disinfected. Also ask whether their work included belongings in the affected areas.

If they have already done the work your home needs, you may not need more cleanup.

### I only found a few droppings. Does my whole home need cleaning?

Not based on that alone.

Tell us where you found them and whether you have seen waste elsewhere. Photos can help us discuss whether a small cleanup fits the job or a visit is needed.

### Can you give me a price from photos?

Some small jobs can be quoted from photos and a phone call.

Larger jobs, blocked areas, or waste mixed into belongings may need a visit. We explain the $145 assessment fee before booking.

### What photos should I send?

If you can take photos without disturbing the waste, send:

- A wide view of the affected space.
- A closer view of what you found.
- A view of nearby boxes, shelves, cabinets, or furniture.

You do not need to move items or enter a hard-to-reach space to get photos.

### How long does cleanup take?

The time depends on the waste, access, and what needs to be moved, cleaned, or removed.

We discuss the expected schedule with your quote.

### What should I do before your visit?

Leave the waste and affected items alone. You do not need to move them for our visit.

Do not sweep or vacuum rodent droppings, urine, or nests. The CDC warns that this can send particles with germs into the air.

If you have already cleaned some of the area, tell us what you did.

[Read the CDC’s cleanup guide](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-pets/rodent-control/clean-up.html).
