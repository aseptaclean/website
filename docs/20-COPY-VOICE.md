## Part 1 — The rules

1. **Contractions in body copy**: don't, isn't, we'll, that's, you're.
   Formality stays in: the Handoff Record artifact, legal/consent text, the
   authority-limit disclaimer (verbatim, never touched).
2. **Mirrored aphorisms ("X, not Y" / "X is not the same as Y"): max ONE per
   page.** The homepage's designated keeper: *"That's the difference between an
   emptied house and one that's actually ready."* All others become plain
   statements.
3. **Break perfect rhythm.** Max one three-item parallel list per section; vary
   list lengths; at least one sentence under six words per section.
4. **Concrete over abstract, dignity intact.** One grounded physical detail per
   abstract section — "a kitchen that hasn't been usable in months," "a garage
   you can't walk through," "mail piled since spring." Never gross-out
   inventories, never shaming detail, never disease language. `01` governs.
5. **One conversational pivot per page max** ("Why? Because…" / "Here's what
   that means:").
6. **Read-aloud gate:** if the owner wouldn't say the sentence on the phone,
   rewrite it.
7. **Untouchable sentences** (claims-adjacent — restructure around, never
   reword): "Nothing leaves the property without your written approval." ·
   all guarantee names/terms · endorsement/insurance wording · SMS consent
   page (verbatim, carrier-review) · Record field text · disclaimer.

## Part 2 — Homepage verbatim replacements (apply EXACTLY)

**Hero H1** — unchanged: *Walk Back Into a Property That Finally Feels Ready.*

**Hero support**
OLD: When a home is still full of belongings, unfinished cleaning, and
decisions nobody has made, it follows you everywhere.
NEW: When a house is still full — the belongings, the cleaning nobody's
started, the decisions nobody's made — it follows you everywhere.

**Hero paragraph**
OLD: Aseptaclean gives you one accountable path through it. You decide what
stays. We clear what you approve, complete the defined deep-cleaning scope,
and document the result so the property can move to its next owner, tenant,
listing, inspection, or family decision.
NEW: Aseptaclean gives you one accountable path through it. You decide what
stays. We clear what you approve, finish the cleaning that's in the scope,
and document the result — so the property can move on to whatever's next: a
sale, a tenant, a family decision.

**Recognition H2** — OLD: You are not looking for someone to carry out a few
bags → NEW: You're not looking for someone to carry out a few bags.

**Recognition lead**
OLD: You are trying to reach the point where the property can be listed,
transferred, rented, returned, inspected, or simply closed without another
unfinished task waiting behind the door.
NEW: You're trying to get the property to the point where it can be listed,
rented, or simply closed out — without another unfinished job waiting behind
the door.

**Recognition body**
OLD: The house may still be full. The cleaning may be more than a normal
cleaner will take on. Family members may disagree about what stays. You may
be trying to manage the property from another city.
NEW: The house may still be full. The cleaning might be more than a regular
cleaner will touch. Maybe the family doesn't agree on what stays. Maybe
you're handling all of it from another city.

OLD: Sometimes it is heavy accumulation that has built up for years.
Sometimes it is an inherited or estate property with decisions no one has
made yet. Sometimes it is a whole-house cleanout on a deadline, or a
property nobody in the family can get to right now.
NEW: Sometimes it's years of accumulation. Sometimes it's an inherited house
full of decisions no one's made yet. Sometimes it's a whole-house cleanout
on a deadline — or a property nobody in the family can get to right now.

**Category contrast** (heading unchanged)
OLD: A hauler may remove the contents. Dust, residue, cabinet debris,
appliance buildup, and unfinished rooms can remain.
NEW: A hauler can take the contents out. What's left behind — the dust, the
residue, the buildup in the cabinets — stays your problem.

OLD: A cleaner may handle the surfaces. The property may need to be
completely empty before the cleaning can begin.
NEW: A cleaner can handle the surfaces, but most won't start until the house
is already empty.

OLD: Aseptaclean manages the handoff condition. Clearing and cleaning are
defined together, completed under the approved scope, and closed with
documentation.
NEW: We do both, under one signed scope — clearing and cleaning defined
together, finished together, and closed out with documentation.

OLD: That is the difference between an emptied property and a property that
is ready for its next handoff.
NEW: That's the difference between an emptied house and one that's actually
ready.   ← the page's single kept aphorism

**Stage 01 Scope**
OLD: We write down what stays, what goes, what gets cleaned, what is
excluded, and what the project will require.
NEW: We write down what stays, what goes, what gets cleaned — and just as
clearly, what's excluded.

**Stage 02 Protect**
OLD: …We do not decide what mattered to your family.
NEW: …We don't decide what mattered to your family.

**"What finished feels like"**
OLD (current long paragraph)
NEW: You unlock the door without bracing for what's still undone. The
approved contents are gone. The rooms in the scope are clean. Anything
important that turned up is set aside and documented. You can send the
realtor, the property manager, or the next tenant inside without handing
them a list of caveats first. The property stops following you around.

**Founder quote** (spoken register)
OLD: "Difficult properties are not handled well through vague promises.
Before work begins, I want you to know what is included, what is excluded,
what happens when something unexpected is found, and what you will receive
when the job is closed. That is the standard I built Aseptaclean around."
NEW: "Difficult properties aren't handled well with vague promises. Before
work begins, I want you to know exactly what's included, what's excluded,
what happens when we find something unexpected, and what you'll receive
when the job's closed. That's the standard I built Aseptaclean around."

**Final CTA**
OLD: Start with what you know. We will help define the rest. / Tell us what
you are looking at, what must remain, and when the property needs to be
ready. Send photos if you have them.
NEW: Start with what you know. We'll help define the rest. / Tell us what
you're looking at, what has to stay, and when the property needs to be
ready. Photos help if you have them.
