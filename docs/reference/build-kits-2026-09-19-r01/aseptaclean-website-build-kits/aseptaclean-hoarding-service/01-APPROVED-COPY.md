Common questions
A few things you may be wondering.
Do I need to sort or clean before you arrive?
No. Contact us with the property as it is. Tell us about items that must stay and any conditions you already know about. We’ll discuss preparation after assessing the work.
Can you help clear a loved one’s home?
Yes. We help families, executors, trustees, and authorized property representatives with homes containing heavy accumulation. We establish who can approve the work and make decisions about the contents.
Will everything be thrown away?
No. We follow the instructions established for the job. Belongings marked to keep or review are separated from contents approved for removal.
Can I manage the cleanup from out of town?
Tell us who can provide access and how you need to receive updates. We’ll discuss communication and approvals before confirming the arrangements for the job.
What if there are droppings, needles, or other contamination?
Tell us about known conditions when you contact us. We assess the cleanup needed and explain what we can take on. Any proposed specialty cleanup has a separate scope and price.
How long does hoarding cleanup take?
The amount of contents, sorting detail, access, and cleaning all affect the schedule. We provide an estimated timeline after assessing the property. Let us know early if you’re working toward a deadline.
How do you price hoarding cleanup?
We assess the property and the tasks you need completed. Your proposal identifies the work and price before you commit. You do not need to identify a hoarding level to request an assessment.
