Common questions
A few things you may be wondering.
What is extreme cleaning?
This service addresses heavy buildup, accumulated waste, and neglected rooms that need more preparation and cleaning than routine service. We assess the condition before confirming the work.
How is this different from a deep clean?
Our detailed deep cleaning focuses on accessible rooms and surfaces. Extreme cleaning addresses heavier buildup and may require waste or contents to be cleared before cleaning can begin.
Do I need to clear the property first?
No. Describe the property as it is. We’ll discuss what needs to be moved, which belongings need attention, and any preparation required before work starts.
Will you throw everything away?
No. Removal is based on what the authorized person approves. Tell us about important papers, valuables, and sentimental belongings so their handling can be addressed in the scope.
Can you remove every stain or odor?
We do not promise complete stain or odor removal. During the assessment, we discuss the materials involved, likely cleaning limits, and whether other work may be needed.
How much does extreme cleaning cost?
The affected areas, buildup, contents, access, and removal requirements determine the price. You receive a written proposal before deciding to proceed. Photos may help us understand the condition, but an on-site assessment may still be needed.
