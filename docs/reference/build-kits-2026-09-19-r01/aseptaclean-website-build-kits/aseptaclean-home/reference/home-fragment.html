Why Aseptaclean

Careful work. Clear Steps.

Before founding Aseptaclean, Matthew Ruiz worked in surgical pathology at Stanford Health Care and pharmaceutical manufacturing at BioMarin. His work required careful handling, contamination control, and accurate records.

He brings those habits to Aseptaclean: follow written steps, pay attention to what is being handled, and document the work.

  • Careful handling: Discuss your belongings and what needs to stay, go, or be reviewed.
  • Clear steps: Review the cleanup plan and price before work begins.
  • Written records: Receive the agreed documentation of the completed work.
