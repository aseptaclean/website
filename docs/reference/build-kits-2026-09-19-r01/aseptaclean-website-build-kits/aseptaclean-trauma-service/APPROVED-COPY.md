Common questions
A few things you may be wondering.
When can cleanup begin?
Call to discuss the property and our availability. Before work begins, we confirm access and authorization. If authorities are involved, the scene must be released first.
What types of incidents do you clean?
We assess cleanup involving blood and bodily fluids after crime scenes, suicides, unattended deaths, accidents, and injuries. Tell us the general situation so we can confirm whether we can help.
Will everything need to be thrown away?
Not necessarily. The material, condition, and extent of contamination affect what can be cleaned. Tell us about important belongings. We explain proposed removal before the authorized person approves it.
Can you help with insurance documentation?
We can provide your written scope, invoice, and the work records included in the job. Ask your insurer what it requires and whether your policy covers the cleanup. Providing records does not guarantee payment.
How much does trauma cleanup cost?
The affected surfaces, extent of contamination, access, and materials requiring removal all affect the price. We assess the work and provide a written proposal before you decide to proceed.
Do I need to send photos?
No. A brief description is enough for first contact. If photos would help, we can discuss them with you. You do not need to enter the affected area or take graphic photos to request help.
