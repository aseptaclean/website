Common questions A few things you may be wondering. Can you remove affected insulation? Yes. Insulation removal can be included in the cleanup plan. We do not install replacement insulation. Not necessarily. We review affected belongings and materials with you, then explain what can be included in cleaning and what is proposed for removal. The condition of each item matters.
