Large cleanouts, heavy waste, and biohazard conditions require a separate assessment and scope. Repairs, pest control, and replacing damaged materials are not part of detailed deep cleaning.
Common questions
A few things you may be wondering.
What is detailed deep cleaning?
It gives extra attention to buildup and areas that routine cleaning may miss, such as cabinet fronts, bathroom fixtures, door frames, and baseboards. Your proposal identifies the tasks included.
How is this different from extreme cleaning?
Detailed deep cleaning focuses on accessible rooms and surfaces. Heavy waste, substantial buildup, or contents that block access may call for extreme cleaning or a separate cleanout. Describe the condition and we’ll help identify the appropriate service.
Do you clean inside appliances and cabinets?
These tasks are included only when listed in your proposal. We discuss access, emptying contents, and any preparation needed before work begins.
Do I need to move everything first?
No. Start by telling us which areas need cleaning. We’ll explain what should be put away and discuss any furniture or belongings that limit access.
Can you remove every stain or mark?
We do not promise that cleaning will remove every mark or restore worn finishes. We discuss visible damage and cleaning limits during the assessment.
How much does detailed deep cleaning cost?
The rooms, buildup, access, and selected tasks affect the price. You receive a written proposal before deciding to proceed. Photos may help us understand the work; an on-site assessment may still be needed.
