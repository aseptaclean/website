Common questions
A few things you may be wondering.
Can you help if the home has hoarding conditions?
Yes. Tell us about the rooms, how much is inside, and any access concerns. We assess the property and define the sorting, removal, and cleaning needed. Suspected contamination is reviewed separately so it can be addressed in the plan.
Do we need to sort everything before contacting you?
No. Tell us what has been decided and what still needs review. The assessment helps define the sorting support and cleanout work to include.
Will belongings be removed without our approval?
Removal follows the agreed scope. Identify items to keep and items needing review before clearing begins, so those decisions are clear.
Can you provide an inventory and photos?
Discuss your documentation needs during the assessment. The scope should identify the items, photos, and records to include rather than assuming a full item-by-item inventory.
How is an estate cleanout priced?
The amount of contents, sorting time, access, removal arrangements, cleaning, and documentation affect the price. You receive a written scope and price before work starts.
