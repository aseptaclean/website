Before you request a plan

Questions that affect scope, authority, and timing

These answers describe the assessment process. The signed scope controls the actual project.

Can I approve the project remotely? +

Yes, when access and decision authority are clear. Scope review, approvals, updates, and closeout can be handled electronically.

Will you throw anything away without asking? +

No. The scope identifies keep, remove, and review areas. Uncertain items are not automatically discarded.

Do you handle animal or organic conditions? +

Yes — cleaning of animal-affected and organic conditions is included under our organic pathogen endorsement, as cleaning within lawful scope. We do not make decontamination or health-safety determinations.

What happens if a hazardous condition is discovered? +

Work stops on that item. We document it, tell you what we found, and refer you to the appropriate licensed specialist.

What can change the price? +

Concealed conditions or customer-requested additions that materially change the work — documented before work proceeds, never as a surprise invoice.

How quickly can the project begin? +

Assessment responses go out within one business day. Scheduling depends on scope, access, and the deadline.
