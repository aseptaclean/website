> When a project requires a dumpster or debris container, Aseptaclean coordinates the applicable
> city-authorized or franchised provider, container placement, and disposal requirements as part
> of the scope. Aseptaclean performs the approved on-site sorting, bagging, staging, clearing,
> loading into the approved container when included, and follow-on cleaning. Aseptaclean does not
> transport customer debris off-site.

rules, permit requirements, and disposal pathways remain city-specific facts and must be verified
before publication.*

# MOUNTAIN VIEW

## Verified city facts

- Recology Mountain View is the only company authorized to provide debris and recycling box
  service and disposal within Mountain View. Using a non-franchised hauler violates the Mountain
  View Municipal Code. *(City of Mountain View, Construction & Demolition; Recology Mountain View)*
- A debris/recycling box contract with Recology is required whether the box sits on private
  property or in the public street. *(City of Mountain View, Temporary Encroachment Permit)*
- A box placed in the public street additionally requires a City Temporary Encroachment Permit,
  for which the City publishes a 30-day processing period. *(same)*
- Street placement rules: not in front of a neighbor's property; at least 6 feet from driveways;
  at least 15 feet from hydrants and crosswalks; not in no-parking, fire, or passenger zones; and
  **not permitted on streets narrower than 36 feet curb-to-curb.** *(same)*
- Recology offers discounted rates for boxes of source-separated recyclables such as wood, metal,
  or drywall. *(City of Mountain View, Developers & Contractors)*
- Residential neighborhoods are swept twice monthly; the City asks that vehicles be moved by
  6:00 a.m. on sweep day. Six streets carry additional posted restrictions: California Street,
  Crisanto Avenue, Foxborough Drive, Latham Street, Ortega Avenue, and Sylvan Avenue.
  *(City of Mountain View, Street Sweeping Schedule)*

## 1. `/service-areas/mountain-view/hoarding-cleanup/`

**H1:** Hoarding Cleanup in Mountain View
**Title:** Hoarding Cleanup in Mountain View | South Bay & Peninsula | Aseptaclean
**Meta:** Owner-operated hoarding cleanup in Mountain View. Written scope before anything is
removed, container coordination through the city's franchised hauler, documented closeout.

**Opening:** *(shared hoarding opening)*

**Local planning notes — Mountain View**

> Recology Mountain View is the only company authorized to provide debris box service and
> disposal within the city. If a project needs a container, it comes from Recology — whether it
> sits on the driveway or in the street.
>
> A box placed in the public street also requires a City Temporary Encroachment Permit, and
> Mountain View publishes a 30-day processing period for it. Placement rules are specific: not in
> front of a neighbor's property, at least six feet from driveways, at least fifteen feet from
> hydrants and crosswalks, not in no-parking, fire, or passenger zones — and not permitted at all
> on streets narrower than 36 feet curb-to-curb.
>
> Neighborhoods are swept twice a month, with vehicles to be moved by 6:00 a.m. on sweep day.

**What this changes for a hoarding project here**

> **Container capacity is a scheduling question, not a same-week one.** A high-volume hoarding
> project usually needs staging capacity. Where a driveway or private area can hold the container,
> the path to a start date is short. Where it can't — a narrow street, no driveway, a multifamily
> property — street placement carries a published 30-day permit process. That gets identified at
> assessment rather than discovered mid-project.
>
> **The 36-foot street rule is the one people don't expect.** Mountain View has residential
> streets below that width, and on those streets a street-placed container isn't slow — it isn't
> available. That changes the removal method itself, not just the timeline, and it's worth knowing
> before a family commits to a date.
>
> **Sorting doesn't wait on the container.** The phases that take the longest on a hoarding
> project — decisions, set-aside, room-by-room sorting — don't depend on a box being present. The
> sequence is built so permit time and sorting time overlap instead of stacking.

**FAQ**

> **How long does the container process take?**
> Mountain View publishes a 30-day processing period for a permit to place a box in the public
> street. If the container remains entirely on private property, that City street-encroachment
> permit is not required; the container still must be arranged through the applicable authorized
> provider. Which placement applies to the property is determined at assessment.
>
> **Who decides what gets removed?**
> The authorized decision-maker identified at assessment. Items marked uncertain or disputed are
> not discarded until that person resolves them.
>
> **What happens if hazardous materials turn up?**
> Work stops on that item. Hazardous materials require separate handling through an authorized
> program and are identified and reported rather than removed as part of a cleanout.

# SUNNYVALE

## Verified city facts

- Sunnyvale provides municipal garbage and recycling service under contract with Specialty Solid
  Waste & Recycling. *(City of Sunnyvale, Recycling and Garbage)*
- Work or material staging in the public right-of-way requires an Encroachment Permit from Public
  Works Engineering. The City's current application instructs applicants to allow a minimum of ten
  working days for review and issuance where revisions aren't required. *(City of Sunnyvale,
  Permit Application for Encroachment)*
- The SMaRT Station, the regional transfer and recycling facility at 301 Carl Road, is located in
  Sunnyvale and operates 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. seven days a week, closed Thanksgiving, Christmas,
  and New Year's Day. Open loads must be covered; Sunnyvale Municipal Code 8.16.160 imposes a fee
  on uncovered loads. *(City of Sunnyvale, SMaRT Station)*
- A vehicle parked in one location for more than 72 hours violates SMC 10.16.120.
- Street sweeping is address-specific; the City publishes a lookup rather than a citywide
  schedule. *(City of Sunnyvale, Street Sweeping Schedule)*

# CAMPBELL

## Verified city facts

- The City of Campbell has an exclusive Franchise Agreement with West Valley Collection and
  Recycling, LLC for dumpsters, debris boxes, and roll-off bins. *(City of Campbell, Garbage &
  Recycling Services; Campbell FAQ)*
- Campbell is a member of the West Valley Solid Waste Management Authority, a joint powers
  authority with Los Gatos, Saratoga, and Monte Sereno.
- Roll-off boxes are available in sizes from 8 to 40 cubic yards. *(West Valley Collection &
  Recycling)*
- A debris box in the public right-of-way may require an encroachment permit depending on
  location; Campbell Public Works determines applicability. *(City of Campbell, Encroachment
  Permits; Campbell FAQ)*
- Between Thanksgiving and Christmas, the City enforces a moratorium on non-emergency street
  construction in the vicinity of Downtown Campbell, and construction hours may be limited before
  city events. *(City of Campbell, Public Works FAQ)*
- Street sweeping runs on a published zone schedule — first and third, or second and fourth,
  Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday depending on zone — and the City asks that vehicles be moved off
  the street on scheduled days. *(City of Campbell, Street Sweeping)*
- Roughly half of Campbell's housing is owner-occupied (50.5%, U.S. Census QuickFacts,
  2019–2023 ACS).
