Before you request a plan

Questions that affect scope, authority, and timing.

Can I approve the project remotely?

Yes, when access and decision authority are clear. Scope review, approvals, updates, and closeout can be handled electronically.

How do you know what should stay?

The signed scope identifies keep, remove, and review areas before any work begins. Uncertain items go to review, not to disposal.

What happens if you find important documents or valuables?

Keys, documents, photographs, cash, jewelry, and similar discovered items are isolated and reported before the job proceeds.

Can you clean after the property has already been emptied?

Yes. Cleaning-only Handoff Finish projects are available for already-emptied properties.

What can change the price?

Property size, contents volume, cleaning condition, access and stairs, disposal requirements, and concealed conditions discovered after work begins.
