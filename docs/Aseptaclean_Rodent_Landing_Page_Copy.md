## Questions before you book.

### My pest-control company offers cleanup. Do I need you too?

You may not need both.

Ask your provider what their cleanup includes:

- Which areas will they clean?
- Will they handle items in those areas?
- What will they remove?
- What will they disinfect?
- How will they help protect nearby spaces?

If they have already done the work your home needs, you may not need more cleanup.

### Will you throw everything away?

No. Some things can be cleaned. Others may need to go or need special care.

We explain the options and discuss what to remove with you.

### What if rodents are still getting in?

Tell us when you call. Rodents can leave new waste after cleanup.

We discuss when to clean based on the pest-control work your home needs.

We do not trap rodents, treat pests, or seal entry holes.

### Are sanitizing and disinfecting the same?

No. Surface sanitizers kill bacteria. Disinfectants can kill certain bacteria and viruses.

What a product can kill depends on its label. It must also be used as directed.

[Read the EPA’s explanation](https://www.epa.gov/coronavirus-and-disinfectants/whats-difference-between-products-disinfect-sanitize-and-clean).

### What should I do before your visit?

Leave the waste and affected items alone. You do not need to move them for our visit.

Do not sweep or vacuum rodent droppings, urine, or nests. The CDC warns that this can send particles with germs into the air.

If you have already cleaned some of the area, tell us what you did.

[Read the CDC’s cleanup guide](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-pets/rodent-control/clean-up.html).
